## Public-proof rules

- The client remains “an established service business” or “an anonymized service-business client” until naming permission is documented.
- Acquisition metrics cannot be used as proof for CRM, Ads, missed-call recovery or automation services. Those pages use a measurement framework until direct outcomes exist.
- Clicks, calls, forms, qualified leads, quoted work and won work are separate funnel stages. Never substitute one for another.
- Screenshots must remove client identity, personal data, phone numbers, addresses, account IDs and sensitive operational figures before publication.
- Source artifacts are retained privately. Public pages may say evidence is available on request only when a sanitized package actually exists.
