# Lead-System Content Briefs

These briefs support the nine commercial services without duplicating their primary sales intent. Every published post should link to its named service and be added explicitly to `BLOG_SERVICE_MAP` in `lib/relatedServices.ts`.

## Pillar 1 — Visibility and demand

### 1. Why a Service-Area Page Ranks in One City but Not the Next

- Query: `service area pages local seo`
- Angle: Diagnose relevance, proof and internal-link gaps city by city; avoid a general Local SEO service pitch.
- Outline: how Google interprets local relevance; thin doorway-page warning; on-page/service proof; internal links; measurement checklist.
- Feeds: `local-seo-google-business-profile`.

### 2. When Paid Search Should Cover an Organic Visibility Gap

- Query: `when to use google ads for local business`
- Angle: A decision framework based on urgency, job value, seasonality and measurement readiness.
- Outline: organic vs paid timing; economic threshold; landing-page fit; conversion tracking; stop conditions.
- Feeds: `google-ads-lead-generation`, then `call-tracking-lead-attribution`.

## Pillar 2 — Website and conversion

### 3. The Mobile Quote Path Test: Can a Customer Contact You in 30 Seconds?

- Query: `contractor website mobile conversion checklist`
- Angle: A task-based audit owners can run from a phone, not a broad website-buying guide.
- Outline: above-fold intent; tap targets; forms; speed on cellular; trust proof; test script.
- Feeds: `lead-generation-websites`.

### 4. One Service, One Decision: When a Dedicated Landing Page Is Worth It

- Query: `service page vs landing page local business`
- Angle: Explain intent separation for a priority service without duplicating the website service page.
- Outline: mixed-intent homepage problem; organic service page vs paid landing page; message match; proof; measurement.
- Feeds: `lead-generation-websites`, `google-ads-lead-generation`.

## Pillar 3 — Lead handling

### 5. You Didn't Lose the Job to a Competitor. You Lost It to Voicemail.

- Query: `missed call text back for contractors`
- Angle: Treat an unanswered phone as a measurable response failure without publishing client-sensitive rates.
- Outline: invisible lead loss; event capture; immediate acknowledgement; callback ownership; success measures.
- Feeds: `missed-call-recovery`.

### 6. The Quote You Sent on Friday Is Already Cold by Monday

- Query: `estimate follow up automation`
- Angle: Separate helpful acknowledgement/reminders from automated selling.
- Outline: why one-touch quotes stall; response sequence; human handoff; CRM status; opt-out and consent.
- Feeds: `lead-follow-up-automation`, `crm-sales-pipeline`.

## Pillar 4 — Tracking and attribution

### 7. Which Ad, Page or Search Produced the Customer?

- Query: `marketing attribution for service business`
- Angle: Plain-language source tracking from click through qualified opportunity, with clear limits.
- Outline: source question; calls/forms/UTMs; CRM association; word-of-mouth gaps; monthly decision report.
- Feeds: `call-tracking-lead-attribution`.

### 8. Your Google Ads Track Clicks. They Don't Track Jobs.

- Query: `google ads conversion tracking contractors`
- Angle: Show why click reporting is insufficient and what a source-to-opportunity loop requires.
- Outline: misleading defaults; call/form conversions; offline conversion import; qualification; budget decisions.
- Feeds: `google-ads-lead-generation`, `call-tracking-lead-attribution`.

## Pillar 5 — Systems and operations

### 9. The Minimum CRM Pipeline a Five-Person Service Business Actually Needs

- Query: `simple crm pipeline for contractors`
- Angle: Define the smallest useful stages and ownership rules instead of comparing software brands.
- Outline: new lead; contacted; qualified; quoted; won/lost; next action; stale-opportunity review.
- Feeds: `crm-sales-pipeline`.

### 10. Automate the Handoff, Not the Judgement

- Query: `lead workflow automation small business`
- Angle: Decide which transitions are safe to automate and which require a person.
- Outline: event vs decision; data validation; deduplication; failure queue; audit trail; staged rollout.
- Feeds: `custom-lead-systems`, `lead-follow-up-automation`.

## Publishing and consolidation rules

- Create/use five pillar categories; do not create a category for every post.
- Do not index or sitemap a category until it has at least three useful posts.
- Before consolidating current website/Maps posts, export Search Console clicks, impressions, queries, backlinks and conversions by URL. Pick a keeper, merge unique value, then 301 only with evidence.
- New posts require a descriptive service link, at least one related article link and a real published/updated date.
