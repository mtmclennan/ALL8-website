## Five content pillars

1. Visibility and demand: local search, GBP, paid coverage and AI-result discovery.
2. Website and conversion: service-page intent, mobile contact paths, speed and trust.
3. Lead handling: missed calls, response time, follow-up and estimate recovery.
4. Tracking and attribution: calls, forms, UTMs, source-to-opportunity reporting.
5. Systems and operations: CRM pipelines, integrations, automation and constraint analysis.

See `docs/lead-system-content-briefs.md` for the publishing backlog. Existing overlapping website/Maps posts require Search Console URL-level data before consolidation or redirects.
